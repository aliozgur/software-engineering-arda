# Data Analytics and AI Apprenticeship

Second curriculum in this repository. Software Engineering lives beside it under `curricula/software-engineering/`. This path lives under `curricula/data-analytics-ai/`.

> Evidence-driven: **question → measure → model → disclose → review**.

apprenti.dev treats this as its own selectable curriculum (phase 21). Until the app discovers nested manifests, you can still read and review the files here as Git-native curriculum. Learner/mentor overlays for a nested curriculum belong under `learners/<id>/c/apprenti-dev-data-analytics-ai-2026/` once the app writes them.

## Shape (v0.1)

- **19 tasks** across Year 1 (two terms + summer) and Year 2 Term 1
- Sequential progression (`curriculum.json` → `progression: sequential`)
- Python + SQL first; models and LLMs only after baselines and validation
- Years 2 Term 2 through Year 4 are not authored yet

### Year 1 / Term 1 — tables you can trust

Workstation, tidy data, pandas you can trace, descriptive statistics, analytic SQL, ethics and disclosure.

### Year 1 / Term 2 — argument and a first model

Honest charts, missingness and leakage, sampling, disciplined EDA, a supervised model vs a baseline, milestone report.

### Year 1 / Summer

Public dataset investigation and a mentor-chosen claim audit.

### Year 2 / Term 1 — AI as a component

Validation design, text/embeddings, LLM eval (not vibes), retrieval with refusal, system card + harness.

`da…` so ids do not collide in conversation with the Software Engineering `y1t1-…` path. The app still scopes work by curriculum id.
